Good to Know

Studio Questions

Why don't I hear anything?

Browsers wait for a tap before playing audio. Tap any key or pad once and check your device volume is on.

Are these the real instrument sounds?

They are simple synthesized previews. Real instruments sound richer, which is why we set up and test each one.

Can I play in the shop instead?

Yes. Visit our Salem store, open Monday to Saturday, and try any instrument in person.
